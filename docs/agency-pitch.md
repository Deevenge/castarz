# CASTARZ Agency Pitch

## What CASTARZ Is

CASTARZ is a modern casting and talent management platform built for agencies that work with actors, extras, models, and production teams.

It helps an agency move the whole casting process into one clean workflow: posting briefs, receiving applications, reviewing talent profiles, shortlisting actors, sharing options with production, confirming bookings, managing last-minute replacements, and communicating final shoot details.

Instead of juggling WhatsApp chats, spreadsheets, screenshots, email threads, missing headshots, and last-minute confusion, CASTARZ gives agencies one organized place to run casting from start to finish.

## The Problem It Solves

Casting work moves fast, and most agencies still manage it with tools that were not made for casting.

Common problems include:

- Actor profiles are scattered across WhatsApp, PDFs, Instagram, Google Drive, or old spreadsheets.
- Agencies lose time asking actors for the same photos, measurements, availability, and credits again and again.
- Production wants to review talent quickly, but agencies often send screenshots, folders, or messy lists.
- Final selections can become confusing when multiple people are giving feedback.
- Actors cancel bookings, and agencies need emergency replacements fast.
- Agencies need to know which actors are reliable, but cancellation history is usually informal or hard to track.
- Final shoot communication often happens in separate WhatsApp groups with no clean booking record.

CASTARZ is designed to make this process feel professional, fast, and controlled.

## Who It Is For

CASTARZ is for casting agencies, talent agencies, extras agencies, and production-facing agencies that need to manage talent at scale.

It works especially well for agencies that handle:

- Commercials
- TV productions
- Films
- Music videos
- Campaign shoots
- Extras casting
- Featured roles
- Last-minute replacement casting
- Large talent pools

## Core Features

### 1. Agency Casting Briefs

Agencies can create casting briefs with all the important details actors need:

- Production name
- Shoot date and call time
- Location
- Rate
- Age range
- Wardrobe notes
- Wardrobe reference image
- Number of actors needed
- Public or agency-network visibility

Briefs can be saved as drafts, published, edited, closed, or deleted with a reason.

### 2. Actor Applications

Actors can view briefs and apply directly inside CASTARZ.

Agencies then get a structured review board showing who applied, what brief they applied for, and each actor’s current status.

Applications can move through a clear process:

- New
- Standby
- Selected
- Booked
- Rejected
- Cancelled
- Replacement available

This gives the agency a proper casting pipeline instead of scattered messages.

### 3. Actor Z-Cards And Profiles

Each actor has a digital z-card that agencies can review.

A profile can include:

- Headshot
- Bio
- Age range
- Height
- Hair color
- Eye color
- Availability
- Credits
- Portfolio albums
- Formal, casual, commercial, and fitness photos

This helps agencies review actors quickly without asking for the same material repeatedly.

### 4. Agency Review Board

The review board is where agencies make casting decisions.

From one place, agencies can:

- Search applications
- Filter by brief
- Filter by status
- Open a full actor dossier
- Put actors on standby
- Select actors for final cast
- Reject applicants
- Confirm emergency replacements
- Review actor reliability

The workflow is built to feel like a real casting desk, not a generic database.

### 5. Production One-Time Selection Link

Agencies can create a private production review link for shortlisted actors.

Production can open the link without needing an account, review actor cards, open full z-cards, view photos and credits, then submit their preferred choices once.

This is useful when a client, director, producer, or production team needs to choose from the agency’s shortlist.

The agency can:

- Create a production link
- Copy the link
- Regenerate the link if production loses it or makes a mistake
- Retire an old active link when a new one is generated
- See which actors production preferred

When the agency finalizes the brief, production’s preferred actors are already pre-selected, but the agency still has full control to change the final cast.

### 6. Final Booking And Brief Closing

When the agency is ready, it can finalize and close the brief.

The agency chooses the final booked actors, adds the final message, and chooses the communication method:

- WhatsApp group link
- CASTARZ shoot room

Booked actors receive the final booking update, while other applicants receive a professional final selection update.

CASTARZ also creates booking records so the agency has a clean history of who was booked for what.

### 7. CASTARZ Shoot Rooms

For productions that need a cleaner communication space, the agency can create a private shoot room.

Shoot rooms include:

- Agency
- Booked actors
- Brief title
- Final cast list
- Actor z-card summaries
- Messaging
- Read status

This gives production-day communication a more organized home than scattered chats.

### 8. Emergency Replacement Workflow

If a booked actor cannot make the shoot, they can request a replacement with a reason.

The agency receives an inbox alert and can open the replacement workflow.

The agency can then:

- Set when the replacement is needed
- Alert the applicant pool
- See actors who respond as available
- Review their profiles
- Check reliability indicators
- Confirm the replacement
- Update the booking and shoot room cast

This helps agencies move quickly when time is tight.

### 9. Actor Reliability Indicators

CASTARZ helps agencies see whether an actor has a pattern of cancelling bookings.

Agency-only reliability information can show:

- Recent cancellations
- Total cancellations
- Confirmed bookings
- A simple reliability score

This is private to agencies and helps them make better casting decisions without publicly shaming actors.

### 10. Inbox And Notifications

CASTARZ includes notifications for important events such as:

- Connection requests
- Application updates
- Standby updates
- Rejections
- Booking confirmations
- Replacement requests
- Replacement availability
- Replacement confirmations
- Brief deletion
- Brief closure

This keeps agencies and actors aligned without relying only on external messaging.
